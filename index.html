About Me

I am a Unity XR / Interactive Engineer with 5+ years of experience in game development, AR/VR, and interactive installations. I started as a mobile game developer, working on gameplay systems, UI, and performance optimization in Unity, which gave me a strong foundation in real-time systems and interaction design. I later moved into XR development, building AR/VR experiences using AR Foundation, XR Interaction Toolkit, and Meta Quest. Alongside XR, I have built interactive installations such as photobooths, kiosk systems, and hardware-based experiences using Kinect and Arduino. My focus is creating immersive, responsive experiences that connect digital systems with real-world interaction.

My Journey

My journey started with mobile game development in Unity and gradually evolved into building real-time XR/VR experiences and interactive installations. Over time, I shifted from pure game mechanics to creating immersive systems that connect digital content with the real world.
